Privacy

This page explains what happens when you sign up for the Battlerun newsletter.

What we collect

Your email address, when you signed up, and which event or link you signed up from.

Why

To send you Battlerun news and playtest invites. Nothing else. We never sell or share your information.

Where it’s stored

In a private Google Sheet that only we can access, and with our email provider, Kit, which sends the emails.

How to leave

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To have your data deleted entirely, email news@devilinorbit.com.

Age

The newsletter is for people 13 and older.
